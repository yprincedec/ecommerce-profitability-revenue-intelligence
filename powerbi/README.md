# Power BI dashboard

[**Open the Power BI report**](Ecommerce_Profitability_Dashboard.pbix)

The report contains five pages:

1. **Executive Overview** — Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, Profit Margin, monthly sales trend, category performance, and regional profit.
2. **Product & Profitability** — top and bottom products, sub-category profit, category margin, and discount analysis.
3. **Customer Intelligence** — customer segments, sales/profit contribution, and average order value.
4. **RFM Customer Analysis** — RFM segment distribution and recency, frequency, and monetary measures.
5. **Business Insights & Trends** — monthly profit, regional sales vs. profit, discount impact, category margin, and Year/Region/Category slicers.

## Historical data scope

The historical cutoff is **2026-11-06**. Documented historical totals are Sales **$2,153,969.48**, Profit **$273,013.27**, 4,672 distinct orders, 804 customers, and a 12.67% profit margin. These are project-level historical totals; individual visuals can change with slicer selections.

## Interpretation and limitations

- Customer segment labels use project-defined RFM scoring rules.
- Discount patterns are descriptive and do not establish causation.
- The PBIX was reviewed locally, but every internal measure and visual has not been independently audited from the PBIX internals.
- Open the PBIX in Power BI Desktop to inspect and interact with the full report.
