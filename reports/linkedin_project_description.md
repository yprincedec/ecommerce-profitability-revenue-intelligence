## Project title

E-commerce Profitability & Revenue Intelligence

## Description

Built an end-to-end business analytics project to investigate sales performance, product profitability, customer purchasing behaviour, discount patterns, and regional trends using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

Analysed a 10,194-row, 21-column modified/extended Superstore-style dataset. Performed data quality checks, cleaning, exploratory analysis, customer-level analysis, and RFM segmentation. Used SQLite to produce 15 business analysis result tables and developed a five-page Power BI dashboard covering executive KPIs, product profitability, customer intelligence, RFM segments, and business trends.

Historical analysis (through 6 November 2026) covered 9,366 records, 4,672 distinct orders, and 804 customers, with $2.15M in sales and $273.0K in profit (12.67% margin).

## Key work

- Data understanding, cleaning, validation, and exploratory data analysis with Python.
- Category, sub-category, discount-band, regional, monthly, and customer analysis.
- RFM customer segmentation using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary metrics.
- SQLite business analysis with 15 exported result tables.
- Five-page Power BI dashboard with executive, product, customer, RFM, and trend views.
- Documented data-quality anomalies, assumptions, and analytical limitations.

## Tools and technologies

Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, SQLite, Power BI, Google Colab, Jupyter Notebook, Git, GitHub.
