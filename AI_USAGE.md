# Responsible AI-Assisted Development

## Purpose
AI was used as a learning and development assistant during this project. It supported problem framing, technical exploration, troubleshooting, interpretation of analytical concepts, and documentation. It was not treated as an authority for business conclusions, and AI-generated suggestions were not accepted without checking them against the project data and workflow.

## Where AI assistance was used

| Stage | AI assistance | Project owner's responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| Problem framing | Helped break e-commerce profitability into questions about sales, profit, discounts, products, regions, and customer behaviour. | Selected the scope and business questions. |
| Python and data preparation | Explained Pandas operations, suggested cleaning and validation approaches, and helped troubleshoot code. | Ran the notebook, inspected the data, and decided whether transformations were appropriate. |
| Exploratory analysis | Suggested useful comparisons across categories, sub-categories, discounts, regions, and customers. | Calculated metrics and interpreted the actual outputs. |
| Customer analytics | Explained RFM concepts and the logic of Recency, Frequency, Monetary values, and customer segments. | Applied the project-defined rules and reviewed segment outputs and caveats. |
| SQL and Power BI | Helped clarify query logic, KPI definitions, and ways to organize dashboard findings. | Reviewed saved SQL outputs and dashboard figures against the project data. |
| Troubleshooting and learning | Explained errors, alternative approaches, and unfamiliar technical concepts. | Understood the final approach and decided what to use. |
| Documentation | Helped organize methodology, findings, limitations, and project explanations. | Reviewed documentation for accuracy. |

## Representative prompt types
These illustrate the kinds of assistance used; they are not presented as verbatim historical prompts.

- How can I validate missing values, duplicates, date ranges, and key columns?
- How should I compare profitability across categories and discount bands?
- How are RFM Recency, Frequency, and Monetary values calculated and validated?
- Help me understand this Python or SQL error and debug it.

## How suggestions were handled
1. AI suggestions were treated as options to understand and test, not automatically correct answers.
2. Code and analytical steps were executed in the project environment before outputs were used.
3. Findings were based on computed project data, not unsupported AI-generated figures.
4. Known data-quality and reconciliation issues remain documented rather than being hidden by an AI-generated explanation.
5. AI assistance did not replace understanding the calculations, explaining the methodology, or defending conclusions.

## Limitations
- AI can suggest incorrect code, assumptions, or interpretations; outputs require testing.
- Discount and profit relationships are descriptive and do not establish causation.
- Profit prediction and sales forecasting are future extensions, not completed model results.
- This is an analytics project that used AI assistance during development; it is not an AI-powered product.

## What this demonstrates
The project combines AI-assisted learning and troubleshooting with hands-on data preparation, exploratory analysis, customer segmentation, SQL analysis, and dashboard development. The analyst remains responsible for validating outputs, understanding limitations, and explaining business conclusions.
